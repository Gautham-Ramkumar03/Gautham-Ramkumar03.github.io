LiDAR Navigation Stack

Full LiDAR-Based Autonomy Pipeline with NDT Localization

SLAMPerceptionROS2Production

Problem

GPS-only autonomy fails in covered or indoor environments. The existing navigation stack needed a complementary sensing modality that could provide reliable localization where satellite signals are unavailable or degraded.

Approach

Mapping Pipeline

Adapted LiDAR-inertial odometry to the Livox Mid-360’s non-repetitive scanning pattern. The sensor’s unique scan geometry required specific tuning of the odometry pipeline to produce consistent, high-quality maps suitable for downstream localization.

NDT Localization

Integrated and extended Normal Distributions Transform (NDT) scan matching for real-time localization against the pre-built map. Tuned the NDT parameters for the specific map format and real-world deployment environments to achieve reliable convergence.

EKF Fusion

NDT pose estimates are fused with GPS (when available) and IMU data through an Extended Kalman Filter, providing smooth state estimation that gracefully handles transitions between GPS-rich and GPS-denied areas.

Results

LiDAR became a first-class sensing modality in the autonomy stack, significantly improving robustness and enabling fully autonomous operation in GPS-degraded environments including indoor factory floors and covered construction zones.
