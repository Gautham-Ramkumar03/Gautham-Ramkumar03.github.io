Paper 2023 – 2026

Acoustic Fire Extinguisher

Sound-Wave Fire Suppression System

Published

Problem

Conventional fire suppression methods (water, foam, chemical agents) cause collateral damage to electronics, documents, and sensitive equipment. Data centers, server rooms, and confined spaces need a non-destructive alternative.

Approach

Acoustic Suppression Mechanism

Low-frequency sound waves (30–70 Hz) suppress combustion through four mechanisms:

  • Oxygen displacement: pressure waves reduce local O₂ concentration near the flame
  • Flame destabilization: induced turbulence disrupts the combustion reaction
  • Fuel vapor dispersion: acoustic pressure scatters combustible vapors away from the flame front
  • Thermal boundary disruption: lowers flame temperature below the ignition threshold

Key Finding

Discovered the relationship between woofer wattage and optimal frequency: a 60W woofer requires ~60 Hz for extinguishment. Lower wattage demands higher frequency and typically requires an acoustic lens or more powerful vortex tube to concentrate energy.

Results

  • Suppression in under 30 seconds in controlled tests
  • Zero water damage or chemical residue
  • First Prize at SREC Innovate 2023 (45+ competing teams)
  • Published in AIP (February 2026), a peer-reviewed comprehensive review of acoustic fire extinguishing technologies
