What was wrong

The robots follow routes that a person taught them. The first behaviour around an obstacle was the safe one: stop and wait. On a working site that means a robot standing in front of a pallet that is never going to move.

Going around is harder than it sounds on this machine. The machine bends in the middle, so the body carrying the load does not point where the driven wheels point. A plan that is safe for the front can put the back through a stack of bricks.

What the robot can do now

  • Go around. Parked things, and things that move: people, scooters, other machines.
  • Know its own shape. The motions it considers are checked for both halves of the body, not only the front. How the robot knows the angle between them is the subject of the second patent application.
  • Not dither. It passes an obstacle decisively instead of weaving, and it gives way to people walking towards it.
  • Find another way. When the taught route is blocked, a planner looks for a detour the machine can physically drive.
  • Be chosen per job. A mission can run on pure pursuit, which stops and waits, or on MPPI, which goes around.

How

The controller is Model Predictive Path Integral control: it samples many short futures through a motion model of the machine, scores them, and blends the good ones into one command. The motion model is a tractor-trailer model. The route repair is Hybrid A*, which plans in the space of poses the machine can actually reach.

How I know

It runs on the robots, on sites, without a person watching. Before that, avoidance behaviours are developed against recorded obstacles and in a simulation of the articulated machine, where a graze counts as a failure.
