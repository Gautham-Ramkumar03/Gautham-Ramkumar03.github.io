Flagship

Flo Cortex

A hardware-agnostic autonomy stack for mapping, localization and control, running unattended in production. Deployed across India and the UAE.

Read the full story

One stack, five layers

  1. 01MapMappingMap a whole site in pieces and join them into one map.
  2. 02LocalizeLocalizationFind itself on power-on, hold the pose through outages, recover when lost.
  3. 03PerceivePerceptionSeparate ground from obstacles, remember what went out of view, catch what is low.
  4. 04DrivePlanning and ControlGo around obstacles, replan a blocked route, with an articulated body.
  5. 05ShipReliabilityTest it, release it, and keep it running when things go wrong.
