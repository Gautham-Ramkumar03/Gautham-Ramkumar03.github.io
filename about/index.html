About Me

I maintain the autonomy stack at Flo Mobility in Bangalore. Navigation, localization, perception, control: the parts that have to keep working when a robot is out on a site with nobody watching it.

Most of my time goes into research and development on that stack. Something fails in the field, I go find out why, and the fix is usually an assumption that needed to be thrown out rather than a parameter that needed tuning. The projects here are mostly that.

I want to go deeper on the theory behind the systems I already ship. I can make them work. I want to understand them well enough to make them better.
