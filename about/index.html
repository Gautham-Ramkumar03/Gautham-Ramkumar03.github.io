About Me

I lead the autonomy stack at Flo Mobility in Bangalore, and I write most of its mapping, localization and control. It runs on robots in India and the UAE, on sites where nobody is watching them.

My work starts from a field failure. Something breaks on a site, I go find out why, and the fix is usually an assumption that had to be thrown out, not a parameter that had to be tuned. A chain of filters became one factor graph that way. A unicycle model became a tractor-trailer. The projects here are mostly that.

I want to go deeper on the theory behind the systems I already ship: estimation that knows when it is wrong, and control that learns from the people who drive these machines well.
